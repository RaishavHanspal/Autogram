# Autogram shayari library

Simple, relatable shayari in Roman Hindi/Urdu, overlaid at the top of every
photo, carousel slide and reel (stationary). Classic dohe and shers are
public domain and keep the poet's name. Entries marked `Original` were
written for this account; they show `poetry.original_credit` from
config/config.yaml (blank = no name line).

Format: a `### <id> · <poet> · ♥ <score>` heading, a `tags:` line and `>` lines.
Add your own freely; a higher ♥ makes a verse appear more often.
